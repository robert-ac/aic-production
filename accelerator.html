The program

Four stages. One direction.

Ideation→Early Startup→Accelerate→Growth
Stage 01

Ideation

Test the idea before you build the company.

Focus: idea validation · market research · ethics & responsible AI · business model · customer discovery · GTM planning.

Stage 02

Early Startup

Turn a validated idea into a working product with real users.

Focus: MVP · product validation · product-market fit · business model · customer acquisition · startup mentorship.

Stage 03

Accelerate

Build traction and become investor-ready.

Focus: traction · investor readiness · angel investor introductions · funding strategy · business growth.

Stage 04

Growth

For companies with demonstrated traction, AIC supports scale-up strategy and institutional funding readiness — and can facilitate introductions to U.S. and North American investors toward Series A/B/C conversations.

Focus: scale-up strategy · institutional readiness · U.S. investor network · cross-border expansion.

AIC helps founders become investor-ready and connects qualified companies with relevant investors. AIC does not guarantee funding and does not itself provide Series A/B/C capital.
