05 Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions.

What is the Alexander Innovation Centre?

AIC is a Vancouver-based AI community hub connecting business transformation, workforce skills, and entrepreneurship through five strategic pillars: Research & Innovation, Industry Collaboration, Corporate Training, Education & Incubation, and AI Compute Environment & Applied Testbed.

How do founders join AIC?

Founders enter through Founders Lab open membership: join the community, take part in events and workshops, build with mentors and peers, and get discovered. Promising startups can progress into AIC's selective acceleration pathway.

Where should my business start with AI?

Start with a workflow and a business outcome. AIC’s Readiness & Adoption pathway produces a prioritized roadmap, with a one-month roadmap phase in the 2027 model. Your AI champion owns the solution and your organization approves each delivery stage.

Can AIC train my team?

Yes. AIC offers role-based learning for management, general staff, and technical/FDE learners. See the Corporate AI Adoption overview for formats and pricing. View formats & pricing
