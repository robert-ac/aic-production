Building in CanadaFrom Idea to Scale

September 22, 2026 · Innovation Night · Vancouver

An evening of Canadian AI, founder connections, and the official opening of AIC Founders Lab.

From the evening

Founders, investors, operators, developers, AI practitioners, and community leaders from across BC came together to explore how Canadian companies can build, commercialize, and scale.

The evening marked the official opening of AIC Founders Lab, a 9,000+ sq. ft. downtown Vancouver space connecting founders with technology, expertise, and resources.

People and conversations

On stage: Eric Zhao, Rob Goehring of AInBC, Justin Hinchcliffe, David Gratton, Kevin Friel, and Harvey Seifter of the World Ethics Organization. Topics included Canada's AI ecosystem, responsible AI adoption, commercialization, creative industries, and ethics.

A Canadian voice AI milestone

The community also celebrated Alebex STT's #8 worldwide position in Artificial Analysis' streaming speech-to-text accuracy benchmark as of September 21, 2026.
