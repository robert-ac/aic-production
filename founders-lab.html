How it works

One funnel, three levels.

Membership opens the ecosystem to everyone. Acceleration and growth support are earned.

Stage 01 · Open Membership

Join the ecosystem

Anyone suitable for the founder ecosystem can join — like a gym membership for building. Join → participate → build → get discovered.

  • Founder community
  • Events & workshops
  • Peer network
  • Mentors & EIRs
  • Innovation Nights
  • Founder resources
  • Ecosystem connections
Promising startups
Stage 02 · Acceleration — selective

Accelerate what's working

AIC identifies promising companies emerging from the Lab: credible founders, a credible idea, early product, validation, early users, and a real market opportunity. Selected startups get deeper support — mentorship, business strategy, validation, GTM, product-market fit work, investor readiness, and angel investor introductions.

  • Mentorship
  • Business strategy
  • GTM & PMF
  • Investor readiness
  • Angel introductions
  • Early-stage fundraising support
Traction & data
Stage 03 · Growth & U.S. capital access

Scale with North American capital

For companies with demonstrated traction — revenue, user growth, commercial pilots, recurring customers, measurable product-market fit — AIC can support institutional investor readiness and facilitate introductions to larger venture capital through its broader North American ecosystem, toward Series A/B/C conversations.

  • Institutional investor readiness
  • U.S. investor introductions
  • Cross-border capital access
  • Growth-stage strategic connections

Membership provides access to the ecosystem. Acceleration is selective. AIC facilitates access, introductions and ecosystem connections — it does not guarantee funding, and it does not itself provide Series A/B/C capital.

The community

Who you'll meet at Founders Lab.

Founders & builders

People building AI companies

First-time and repeat founders, engineers, and product builders working on real AI products — meeting at workshops, build sessions, and Innovation Nights.

Mentors & EIRs

Experienced operators

Mentors and entrepreneurs-in-residence who have built, scaled, and exited — available to members through the community and to selected startups through acceleration.

Investors

Angel & venture connections

Investors meet AIC startups through curated introductions and community events — when companies are ready, not before.

The wider ecosystem

Employers, researchers & students

Founders Lab connects into the whole AIC ecosystem — the Learn and Lab pillars, employer partners, and Vancouver's applied AI community.
